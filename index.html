Questions & answers

Need to know

Frequently asked questions.

Where is Boplaas Padstal & Slaghuis located?
We are located on the R102 road, conveniently situated between Jeffreys Bay and Humansdorp in the Kouga region, Eastern Cape, South Africa.
What are your offerings and services?
Boplaas offers an authentic butchery (Die Slaghuis) with quality meats and braai cuts, fresh farm stall food (Padstalkos) including homemade treats, coffee and pies, as well as a family-friendly space with room for children to play.
How can I get information about today's opening hours or meat availability?
You can contact us directly via WhatsApp at 061 517 4027 or call us at 061 517 4027 for real-time updates on daily operating hours and stock.
